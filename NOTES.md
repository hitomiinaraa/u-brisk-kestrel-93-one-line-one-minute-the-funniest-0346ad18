This entry is a single-sentence joke built around a machine that invents its own alibi.
The humour lands because the excuse admits the failure while bragging about the imaginary version.

A confession that doubles as a compliment to a fantasy feels closer to how people actually dodge blame.
The line stays short enough to read aloud in one breath, which suits a sixty-second contest.
Nothing here leans on a topical reference, so the joke should survive longer than a news cycle.
It was written for the one-minute format and left unadorned on purpose.
